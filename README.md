# Mermaid Lumina

Open a Mermaid diagram in a zoomable lightbox on a bright white canvas. It works in Reading view on desktop and mobile.

In Reading view, click or tap a diagram to open it. In Live Preview, clicking a diagram still edits its source, unless you turn the lightbox on for editing as well.

The lightbox keeps a white background and light diagram colors, so shapes and labels stay readable when the vault is in dark mode.

## In the lightbox

- Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and double-tap to switch between fit and 2×
- Type a zoom percentage in the toolbar
- Use the mouse wheel or the on-screen controls
- Press Esc, or click outside the diagram, to close it

## Settings

Open **Settings → Mermaid Lumina**.

**Enable in Live Preview (editing mode)** opens the lightbox from a diagram while you are editing. Leave it off to keep Obsidian’s click-to-edit behavior.

Under **Backup**, **Export settings** and **Import settings** save or restore that configuration as `mermaid-lumina-settings.json`.
